AuthorityCurrent DOP/referral

Claims

IntimationRegister and identify policy/claim
CoverageValidity, insured, peril/event, exclusions
DocumentsCheck required documents and deficiencies
Survey/InvestigationWhere applicable
AssessmentLoss, admissibility, deductions, excess
AuthorityDOP / referral / exceptional case
DecisionSettlement, partial settlement or reasoned rejection
ClosurePayment, communication, system/MIS and record

Policy Servicing

RenewalDue policy, terms and premium
EndorsementRequested change and supporting proof
CorrectionRecord/policy verification
CancellationTerms, reason and refund calculation
CustomerClear written communication

Complaint / Grievance

RegisterCapture issue and policy/claim reference
ExamineFacts, records and applicable wording
CoordinateObtain response from concerned function
ReplyReasoned, source-based response
EscalateUse prescribed mechanism where unresolved
CloseRecord resolution and evidence

AO Checklists

File examination

  • What exactly is the decision required?
  • Chronology and facts clear?
  • Applicable policy/rule identified?
  • Required documents complete?
  • Survey/investigation/expert report needed?
  • Financial impact calculated and supported?
  • Current DOP/authority checked?
  • Any circular amendment / special instruction?
  • Reasoned proposal recorded?
  • Next action and due date recorded?

Claim examination

  • Policy active and correct?
  • Insured/vehicle/property/event matched?
  • Coverage/peril checked?
  • Exclusions/conditions/warranties checked?
  • Intimation and documents complete?
  • Survey/assessment/investigation reviewed?
  • Quantum and deductions checked?
  • Fraud/red flags considered under applicable process?
  • DOP/referral checked?
  • Decision communication and system closure planned?

DOP / Authority Desk

Use this screen as the authority checkpoint before taking an approval or settlement decision.

1. Identify transactionUnderwriting, claim, endorsement, refund, settlement, repudiation, etc.
2. Check current OICL DOPUse the latest internal DOP / authority matrix applicable to the office and function.
3. Check referralIf outside delegated authority, identify the prescribed referral/escalation channel.
4. Record authorityNote the DOP clause/reference in the file note or system record as required.
Important: A current internal OICL DOP/authority matrix was not publicly verified here. Do not rely on guessed monetary limits. Add only the latest authorised internal DOP available to Prachi.
