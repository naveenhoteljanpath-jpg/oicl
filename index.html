Daily work processes and decision flows

Motor Claim Workflow

1. Receive ClaimCall, email, or portal submission
2. Verify DetailsPolicy, dates, coverage match
3. Assess ClaimReview documents, survey if needed
4. Authorize/RejectPer DOP and policy terms
5. SettlementProcess payment or communicate

Fire Claim Workflow

1. Claim NoticeReceive notice of loss
2. VerificationPolicy validity and coverage
3. SurveyPhysical inspection if required
4. SettlementAuthorized amount based on loss

Proposal Underwriting Workflow

1. Receive ProposalCollect completed form
2. Review InfoVerify applicant details
3. UnderwriteAssess risk per guidelines
4. Approve/RejectIssue decision, communicate
5. Issue PolicyGenerate and deliver policy
